Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about booking a taxi.

How do I book a taxi?

Fill in the Booking Details form above or choose a popular route. We will contact you to confirm your request.

Can I book a one-way trip?

Yes. The homepage form is set up for one-way trips. For round trips, local rides, or airport transfers, use the full booking form.

Are the route prices final?

Route cards show starting fares. The final fare is confirmed based on your pickup details, vehicle choice, and trip requirements.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve cities and popular destinations across Tamil Nadu, including Thiruvannamalai, Ooty, Kodaikanal, and nearby travel hubs.
